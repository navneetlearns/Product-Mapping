02Enhancements implemented

Four improvements, now in place

01
Invoice Reprocessing
Introduced the invoice reprocessing functionality for Man-in-the-Middle in zotok panel, allowing users to reprocess invoices either individually or in bulk, improving operational efficiency and reducing manual effort.
02
Automatic Rejection of Unclear Images
Implemented automated rejection of unclear or illegible invoice images directly on WhatsApp, enabling users to identify image quality issues immediately and upload clearer images for processing.
03
Original Invoice Link for Duplicate Detection
Enhanced duplicate invoice identification by providing a direct link to the original invoice, allowing users to quickly access and verify the previously submitted invoice.
04
Increased Page Display Limit
Enhanced the pagination functionality in the Zotok panel by increasing the number of entries displayed per page from 40 → 100, making it easier for users to review and manage larger volumes of invoice records with fewer page navigations.
