Summary

What changed and what it showed.

  • The mapping agent was first measured on prod data in LangSmith: 1.00 when the title carries the SKU, 0.94 on plain titles, 0.90 on brand-named titles.
  • That eval moved into Sentinel, against a deployed eval endpoint, and then switched from saved search results to searching the live catalog at run time.

  • The remaining misses trace mostly to QA catalog data (missing or 100× MRPs) and near-identical sibling products.

How the scoring works

Two questions per line: was the status right, and was the SKU right. The search is graded on its own as well.

  1. Status

    Mapped / mapped-with-brand-not-found / unmapped, compared with the recorded result.

  2. SKU

    The chosen product against the expected one, with equivalent catalog items counted as correct. A line counts only when it was not left unmapped.

  3. Search

    Is the expected product among the top 5 candidates, and at what rank (top-1, top-5, MRR)? Did it come in by SKU or by name, and did its MRP match the line's?

  4. Totals

    Every line scored the same way; totals come from each run's own report. The QA status figure is computed from that report against the labelled statuses.
